I'm Ken Zhou (周健). I run a home textile and mattress business, and I build AI systems for my own work.

This blog is where I write things down properly. Three things come up most often:

- **AI** — how I actually use it in daily work, what holds up, and what turns out to be noise.
- **Business** — operating a real company: products, stores, cross-border sales, and the cost of getting things wrong.
- **Life** — health, reading, and the long, unglamorous work of getting better at both.

I'm not a programmer by training. Most of what I know about software came from building things with AI and watching them break. That's part of why I write: the notes I needed were rarely the ones that existed.

If something here saves you a week, it did its job.
